Project Overview

What Godrej Verano actually is, in plain terms

Godrej Verano is the new residential project from Godrej Properties in Sector 63A, Gurugram, sitting just off Golf Course Extension Road. Godrej bought this 11.36 acre parcel outright in March 2026 for close to ₹1,000 Cr and has guided the market to ₹4,500 Cr in sales from it. That is the context for the pricing.

The plan is deliberately low density for this corridor. Four high rise towers of roughly 36 to 40 floors, four apartments on every floor, and around 624 homes in total. Compare that with 1,000 plus unit projects nearby and you understand why the open area comes to more than 80 percent.

The theme is Miami style resort living, which in practice means three separate clubhouses (Godrej calls it the TriBay concept), about 2 lakh sq ft of club built up area, big water bodies and a lot of landscaping. Apartments are 3 BHK, 3 BHK with servant room, 4 BHK with servant room and 5 BHK with servant room, from 2,150 sq ft to 3,900 sq ft. Penthouses go up to around 6,500 sq ft.

The project received its HRERA registration on 11 August 2026 and the land is licensed under License No. 129 of 2025. Construction is slated to start in September 2026 and the RERA registered completion date is in 2033. Godrej is currently collecting EOIs ahead of the formal allotment.

What is not in the price

  • PLC for park facing, corner or higher floors (standard in Gurgaon, expect ₹250 to ₹600 per sq ft)
  • Floor rise charges above a certain floor
  • Covered car parking (two slots usually bundled, extra slot chargeable)
  • Club membership and IFMS
  • GST at 5 percent on under construction homes
  • Stamp duty (7 percent male, 5 percent female, 6 percent joint in Haryana) and registration fee

Quick cost example

For a 3 BHK at ₹5.91 Cr, stamp duty alone is about ₹41 lakh for a male buyer or ₹29.5 lakh if the flat is registered in a woman's name. GST adds roughly ₹29.5 lakh. So budget around ₹6.7 to ₹6.9 Cr all in before PLC and parking. We will give you the full working sheet for the unit you shortlist.

Micro market

Why Sector 63A, and why now

Golf Course Extension Road is the stretch from Sector 55-56 down to Sohna Road. It got its road widening done, the Sector 55-56 metro is live and the extension towards Vatika Chowk is under construction. Office stock has moved in behind it, so the area now has its own workday population instead of being a bedroom suburb for Cyber City.

Sector 63A specifically was one of the last sectors on the road to be licensed for group housing. It is a quieter pocket than Sector 65 or 66, one road off the main artery, which is exactly what you want for a home. The neighbours are DLF, Sobha, Emaar and M3M, so there is no question about the quality of the surrounding development.

On prices: resale in finished towers like Emaar Marbella and Ireo Victory Valley on this stretch is in the ₹18,000 to ₹22,000 per sq ft range in 2026, while new launches are quoting ₹25,000 to ₹30,000. Verano at ₹27,490 is priced in line with new supply, with the usual argument that you are paying for a 2033 product at 2026 rates.

Payment Plan

Godrej Verano payment plan and booking process

1

EOI of ₹10 lakh

Refundable cheque or transfer in favour of Godrej Properties. This gets you a place in the allotment queue and a priority slot for unit selection. Fully adjusted against the booking amount.

2

Unit selection and booking

On launch day you pick your tower, floor and facing in your EOI order. Booking amount is capped at 10 percent of the price by RERA until the agreement for sale is registered.

3

Agreement for sale

Signed within 60 days of booking. Stamp duty on the agreement is nominal. Home loan sanction happens at this stage. All major banks have pre approved the project.

4

20:5 construction linked plan

Godrej has indicated a 20:5 plan for this launch, which means a 20 percent tranche early followed by smaller construction linked instalments. We will share the written milestone schedule before you pay anything beyond the EOI.
